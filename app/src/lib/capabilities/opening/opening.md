# opening

Where a thing of a given kind is opened.

The companion to [`naming`](../naming/overview.md). Four surfaces ask it, and
four copies of the branch would be four places for one rule to be wrong.

**A target, not a call.** A caller may want to know whether a kind is openable at
all, and a surface that only ever received a side effect could not ask.
**Nothing is a real answer** — a file, a finding and a connector have no screen
that holds them.

**It answers nothing yet.** The views that call it name what it owes them.
