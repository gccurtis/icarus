# inspecting

Which lens answers for a thing, and what that lens is about.

The third of the id capabilities, beside [`naming`](../naming/overview.md) — what
an id is called — and [`opening`](../opening/overview.md) — where it opens. This
one answers what a selection asks: something was picked out of a list, so which
panel knows how to read it.

**A key and a selection, never a call.** Deciding and doing are separate acts.

**It answers from sample rows.** No `api/` yet.
