## The shape of a runtime

A resource runtime is the client's copy of one document, deck or sheet: `body`, `revision`, `sync`, a `buffer` of ops not yet sent, and undo and redo stacks. [[file:app/src/lib/model/client/document-runtimes/definition.svelte.ts]] is the document one; the slide deck and spreadsheet runtimes have the same seven methods. `attach(id)` creates or revives a runtime, reads the leader body through `readDocumentBody`, and re-reads it every `syncEveryMs` while attached — but only when the runtime is settled, with nothing buffered and nothing in flight, so a keystroke during a round trip is never overwritten. `release(id)` moves the runtime to a settling map and flushes it once more; `releaseAll` is what `close()` on the client model calls.

`apply(ops)` pushes the ops on the undo stack, clears redo, appends to the buffer and schedules a flush: immediately when the buffer reaches `afterOps`, otherwise after `afterMs` of quiet. `undo` and `redo` produce ops too — the inverse of the last entry — and buffer them the same way, so history is just more ops.

## Thresholds

The numbers come from `revisions.yaml` through the published keys: flush after 50 ops or 2000 ms, sync every 5000 ms. Before sending, [[file:app/src/lib/model/client/document-runtimes/methods/flush/coalesce.ts]] folds the buffer: consecutive `text` splices on one atom compose into one splice, a later `set` on a path absorbs an earlier one keeping the original `was`, and a splice that composes to nothing is dropped — provided no op on related ground sits between them. Fifty keystrokes in one word become one op.

[[file:app/src/lib/model/client/document-runtimes/methods/flush/flush.ts]] then sends a change set — `resourceId`, `baseRevision`, `ops`, and the `touched` paths — through `submitDocumentChanges`. Accepted: take the new revision, re-sync. Refused as `stale`: [[file:app/src/lib/model/client/document-runtimes/methods/flush/rebase.ts]] adopts the leader's revision, puts the refused ops back in front of the buffer, marks `rebasing`, and the flush retries exactly once. Refused for any other reason, or refused again: the buffer is dropped, the leader body is re-read and the runtime is marked `needs-review`. A thrown error puts the ops back and marks `error`. One flush is in flight at a time (`pendingFlush`).

## The server side

[[file:app/src/lib/capabilities/document/api/submit-document-changes/submit-document-changes.ts]] is the mirror image: find the leader snapshot (`role: "leader"`, one per resource), refuse `stale` unless `baseRevision` equals its revision, apply the ops with [[file:app/src/lib/capabilities/document/api/submit-document-changes/apply-ops.ts]] and refuse `unresolved` if any op cannot apply, then in one pass create a `documentChangeSets` row at `revision + 1`, update (or create) the leader snapshot, and stamp `updatedAt` and `updatedBy` on the document. There is no merge: a stale change set is refused whole and the client rebases. The `resources.*` keys in `revisions.yaml` — rebase window, consolidation, history depth, checkpoint interval — describe tiers nothing implements yet; the change set is written with `tier: "recent"` and nothing consolidates it.
