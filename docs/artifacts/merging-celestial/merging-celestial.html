Orange stops being reserved.

--theirs, not --ours. During a rebase the flags are inverted from the intuition: you are replaying your commits onto main, so --ours is main and --theirs is your branch. Getting this backwards here silently discards the whole rewritten file and leaves a page that still typechecks. Open the file after resolving and confirm it is the .data-table version.
