## The vocabulary

`components/authored/chart` is one vocabulary with two renderers. [[file:app/src/lib/components/authored/chart/chart.svelte]] is layerchart — `BarChart`, `LineChart`, `AreaChart`, `PieChart`, `ScatterChart` over SVG — chosen because SVG is painted by the same custom properties as everything else and serialises, which is what `copyChart` relies on to make a PNG. [[file:app/src/lib/components/authored/chart/plot/layout.ts]] is the family's own layout, with no drawing in it, used by `PlotBars` and `PlotPie` for the cases where a library's sealed geometry cannot be corrected: labels landing on top of one another, a selectable segment, a horizontal stack. `ChartMekko`, `ChartColors` and `ChartGrowth` are the remaining parts; `growth.ts` holds `cagr`, `asPercent` and `elementOverElement`.

## From spec to marks

[[file:app/src/lib/components/authored/chart/chart-spec.ts]] says what a chart *is*: a `type` (bar, line, area, scatter, pie, mekko), `data`, an `x` and `y` `AxisSpec`, `series`, and `settings`. `settingsFor(type)` answers which settings mean anything for a type, so a panel shows no orientation control beside a pie. `layoutBars(data, categoryField, series, layout, horizontal, size)` turns that into `Mark`s — one addressable rectangle per bar or segment, with an id derived from `<category>::<seriesKey>` rather than from position, so a selection survives sorting and hiding. Horizontal is chosen up front and every box is built in final coordinates rather than transposed afterwards. `ticksFor` picks round ticks so two charts of one quantity are comparable; `placeValueLabels` centres a stacked segment's figure in its own segment and drops a label that does not fit rather than overlapping it; `placeTotalLabels` puts one figure over each band. [[file:app/src/lib/components/authored/chart/chart-selection.svelte.ts]] holds which mark ids are selected.

## Series colour

[[file:app/src/lib/components/authored/chart/palette.ts]]: six role tokens in order — accent-1, accent-2, interactive, intelligence, success, attention — wrapping rather than running out. Danger and inactive are deliberately absent, because red means failed and grey means switched off everywhere else in the application. They are written as `var(--color-…-fill)`, the Tailwind-side names the integration maps from the tokens, which is why a chart follows a theme switch like everything else.
