## This thread

Every turn as a row: the prompt, when it was asked, and what it produced.
Selecting one brings it to the centre.

**Shows**

- *Was the coordination study ever redone?* — 10:21 · 2 findings proposed — now
- *Why did Feeder 12 fail twice?* — 10:14 · 1 finding accepted — 7m
- *What does the event log show for January?* — 10:02 · no findings — 19m

Each turn is a prompt and what it produced. That summary is what makes the list
scannable — a list of prompts alone would not say which turn mattered.

**Needs** — the thread's turns, each with its prompt, its time, and counts of what
it produced.

**Open** — no branch provenance. Selecting an earlier turn and asking something
new has no defined relationship to the turns after it.
