What the inspector shows when nothing is selected. It has one job beyond identity:
say what to do next, because an editor with no toolbar and an empty inspector
gives no instruction at all.

## This document

**Shows** — `Title · Q3 Resilience Memo`, `Pages · 5`, `Words · 1,204`, `Saved · All changes saved`

**Needs** — the document record, pagination and word count.

## Nothing selected

One sentence: click a block, a formula, the header or the footer to change it —
and Insert is in the panel.

This is the compensation for removing the toolbar. Without it, a new document
presents no visible way to do anything.

**Needs** — nothing.
