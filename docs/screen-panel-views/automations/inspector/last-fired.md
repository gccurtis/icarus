## What Started means

The task was created. Whether it finished is the task's own story, and a later
failure never rewrites this line.

This is the most important sentence on the screen. "Success" would be a lie about
a dispatch, which is why the word is Started — and why the distinction is a
section rather than a tooltip.

**Needs** — nothing.

## The task it made

The way from the dispatch to the work. Starts collapsed.

**Shows** — *Summarise overnight outage reports* — Running · step 3 of 5

**Needs** — the dispatched `AgentTask`, which opens the
[shared task lens](../../_shared/inspector/copilot-task.md).

**Open** — a fire that could not start made no task, so this section is empty
exactly when someone most wants to know why. The reason has to carry the weight,
which is why it is on the Summary above.
