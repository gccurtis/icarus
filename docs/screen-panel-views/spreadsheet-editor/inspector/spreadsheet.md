## This spreadsheet

**Shows** — `Title · Outage Cost Model`, `Used range · A1:G6`,
`Populated cells · 42`, `Saved · All changes saved`

**Needs** — the spreadsheet record, its extent, and its sync state.

## Nothing selected

One sentence: click a cell to see what is in it; selecting a range offers
formatting, naming and merge.

The same compensation the other editors make for having no toolbar.

**Needs** — nothing.

## Calculation

Icarus's formula engine is the only calculation authority, and every formula
reads its inputs when it runs.

Stated here because the grid is the one surface where people expect a
recalculation command, and its absence needs an explanation rather than a
missing button.

**Needs** — nothing.
