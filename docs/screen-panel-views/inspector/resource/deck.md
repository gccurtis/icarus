## Nothing selected

One sentence saying what to do: click an element to change it, shift-click for
several, and new/duplicate/delete are at the top of the Slides panel.

The same compensation the document editor makes for having no toolbar — without
it, an empty canvas offers no instruction.

**Needs** — nothing.
