A summary and a way back in, not a transcript. The transcript belongs in the
Copilot's own surface.

## Conversation

Which agent, what it is called, how long it has been going.

**Shows** — `Relay coordination history`, `Agent · Grid Analyst`, `Turns · 14`

**Needs** — `PersonaChat` title, persona, and turn count.

## Latest

The last thing said, so you can tell whether this is the thread you meant.

**Shows** — "The filings index lists a 2019 study and no successor. Two sources."
— Grid Analyst · 14:02

**Needs** — the most recent `Message` in the thread, with its author and time.

## Actions

**Continue** reopens the thread in the composer. **Start a task from this** turns
a conversation into work.

**Open** — what a task inherits from the conversation it came from is undefined.
The prompt, the scope, and the history are three different answers.
