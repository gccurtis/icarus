## What this is for

The section's purpose, so the five are distinguishable without a manual. Focus is
not Approach and Approach is not Output, and nothing about the names makes that
obvious.

**Shows** — "What to concentrate on and what to leave alone."

**Needs** — static per-section explanations.

## Text

The content, editable.

**Shows** — "Concentrate on outage causation from field evidence: relay logs,
event sequences, weather records. Leave cost allocation and rate design to the
Filing Editor."

**Needs** — the persona's definition field for this section.

## Cost

What it costs to have written it.

**Shows** — "168 characters, sent on every call this Persona makes."

**Needs** — a character count.

**Open** — characters are a proxy for tokens, and a bad one for non-Latin text.
Whether to show tokens instead needs deciding, and would require a tokeniser in
the client.
