## Open

Each thread with the cell it is on, so a comment about C2 is findable without
hunting for a marker in the grid.

**Shows** — Mira Jain on C2 — "@ana corrected total or the old one?" — 1d

**Needs** — `Comment` threads anchored to this spreadsheet, with address, author,
body, time and mention detection.

**Open** — persisted anchors are the spreadsheet, a cell, or a text range. A
range, a chart, a row or a column cannot be commented on, which rules out the
most natural thing to say something about — a column of numbers.
