# Print

| --- | --- | --- |
| Print | Getting a grid onto paper | Page setup · Area and repeats · Show |

A grid has no natural page, so every one of these settings is a decision someone
has to make. That is why print is a view rather than a dialog at the moment of
printing.

## Page setup

**Shows** — `Paper · Letter`, `Orientation · Landscape`, `Scale · Fit to 1 page wide`

**Needs** — print settings on the spreadsheet.

## Area and repeats

What to print, and what to repeat on every page so a table stays readable past
page one.

**Shows** — `Print area · A1:G6`, `Repeat rows · 1:1`, `Repeat columns · A:A`

**Needs** — a stored print area and repeat ranges.

**Open** — these are ranges, and ranges shift when rows and columns are inserted.
They belong in the structural-rebase contract with everything else that holds an
address.

## Show

Starts collapsed.

**Shows** — `Gridlines · off`, `Headings · off`

**Needs** — the two flags.
