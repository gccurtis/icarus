# Recent

| View | What it is for | Sections |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recent | What you had open lately, and what changed lately | Today · Yesterday · Earlier |

Grouped by day. Every kind appears, including Research threads, because "what was
I doing" does not respect the distinction between a resource and a thread.

## Today

**Shows** — *Q3 Resilience Memo* — 4m, *Board Update — October* — 2h

**Needs** — recently-opened comes from local tab history; recently-updated comes
from `updatedAt`. Neither is a persisted favourite, and the two are different
lists that this view merges.

**Open** — merging them means a document you never opened can appear under
"Recent" because someone else edited it. Whether that is wanted needs deciding.
