## Open

Threads still being worked, with their mode implied by the icon and their size by
the turn count.

**Shows**

- *Why did Feeder 12 fail twice?* — 3 turns · asked just now
- *Is Eastbrook exposed the same way?* — 4 turns
- *Undergrounding beats vegetation management* — 22 turns

**Needs** — the project's threads with mode, turn count and last activity.

## Answered

Threads that reached a conclusion, with what they produced.

**Shows** — *What did the 2024 study assume?* — 6 turns · 2 findings

**Needs** — a thread state, and per-thread accepted-finding counts.

**Open** — what makes a thread "answered". If it is derived from its anchoring
question's status, then a thread has no state of its own and the section is a
projection. If it has its own state, someone has to set it.

## Panel furniture

**New thread** in the action row, and a search over threads.
