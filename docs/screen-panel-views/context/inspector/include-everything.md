## Rule

What it means, in words: every resource in this project, including anything
created after this Context was saved.

The "including anything created after" clause is the whole point and cannot be
left implicit.

**Needs** — nothing beyond the term itself.

## Right now

**Shows** — `Matches · 248`

**Needs** — a live count of project resources.

## Retrievable

How much of that can actually be searched. Starts collapsed.

**Shows** — `Indexed · 231`, `Nothing indexed · 17`

**Needs** — indexed counts over the match set.

## Actions

**Move to Take out** flips it to the other half. **Remove** deletes the term.

**Open** — "everything in this project" on the Take out side means the Context is
empty by construction. Whether that is refused or allowed needs deciding — it is
the one composition that produces a zero-member scope on purpose, which is
exactly what the model cannot currently express.
