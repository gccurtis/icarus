A question outlives any thread that works on it. This lens is about the question,
not about the conversation.

## Question

**Shows** — `Text · Why did Feeder 12 fail twice?`, `Status · Investigating`,
`Parent · Why do feeders fail repeatedly?`

**Needs** — `Question` text, status and parent.

**Open** — status is set by a person. Nothing derives it, and nothing should: a
question with three accepted findings can still be open.

## Linked hypotheses

The ideas offered as answers.

**Shows** — *Relay coordination was never redone*; *Vegetation was the shared
cause*

**Needs** — `ResearchLink` rows from hypotheses to this question.

## Accepted findings

What has been established that bears on it. Starts collapsed.

**Shows** — *Relay pair mis-coordinated since 2024*

**Needs** — `ResearchLink` rows from findings to this question.

**Open** — a finding can bear on a question directly or through a hypothesis.
Whether this section shows both, and how it distinguishes them, is unsettled.
