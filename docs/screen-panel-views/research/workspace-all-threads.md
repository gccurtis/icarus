There is one Research tab, not one per thread. This is how you get between them.

## Header

**Shows** — "Research" over "Every line of enquiry in this project. Opening one
brings it to the centre — there is one Research tab, not one per thread.", and
**New thread**

**Needs** — nothing beyond the create route. The subtitle carries the tab rule,
which is otherwise invisible.

## Filters

**Shows** — a search over threads and findings, then `All` · `Questions` ·
`Hypotheses` · `Open-ended`

**Needs** — the thread query to accept a text search and a mode filter.

## Threads

Each thread as a row: what it is, what job it has, how much work is in it, what it
produced, and when it was last touched.

**Shows**

| Thread | Job | Turns | Findings | Last asked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Why did Feeder 12 fail twice? | Answer one question | 3 | 1 accepted · 2 proposed | just now |
| Undergrounding beats vegetation management | Test an idea | 22 | 9 accepted | 2 days ago |
| Winter storm precedents | Look around | 9 | 4 accepted | 1 week ago |

*Findings* is the column that says whether a thread went anywhere. Turns alone
measure effort, not result.

**Needs** — the project's threads with mode, turn count, accepted and proposed
counts, and last activity.

## Note

One line under the table: a thread has one job, chosen when it starts — look
around, answer one question, or test one idea. That is what keeps an enquiry from
becoming a chat room.

**Needs** — nothing.
