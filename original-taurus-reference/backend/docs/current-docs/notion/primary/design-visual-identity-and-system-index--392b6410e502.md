> **Governing outcome:** Taurus should feel like **a quiet workspace with infinite volume**—a weightless futuristic citadel formed from and resting within a sea of clouds. It is smooth, easy to read, easy to stay with, and responsive enough to become a natural extension of thought. Its beauty comes from total formation, organization, curvature, and refinement rather than ornament or visible complexity.
# Authority order
When visual documents disagree, use this order:
1. Latest explicit founder decisions recorded under Taurus Yesod.
2. [Design - Emotional & Aesthetic Doctrine](https://app.notion.com/p/392b6410e5028150b8d3fa2a8aa95895).
3. Binding accessibility standards and validated user evidence.
4. [Design - Color, Light & Material System](https://app.notion.com/p/64f538abd9ec4a8d82591c66c7e17a49).
5. [Design - Visual Language, Tokens & Resource Signatures](https://app.notion.com/p/39ab6410e50281798739fa3a9e8931ac).
6. Active specialized design documents.
7. Resource-specific specifications and validated implementation constraints.
8. Existing code and screenshots as implementation evidence—not automatic design authority.
An existing value does not become canonical merely because it is implemented. A new idea does not become canonical merely because it is evocative. It must support processing fluency, sustained comfort, realistic work, product coherence, and accessibility.

# Settled decisions
## Emotional character
- Taurus is smooth, welcoming, comfortable, and commercially credible.
- The singular metaphor is a futuristic citadel formed from and resting within a sea of clouds.
- The citadel is luminous and weightless despite its scale. It may reflect pearl, warm tan, cool metallic-blue, or restrained sunset color; it is not defined by whiteness.
- Beauty comes from organization, proportion, curvature, refinement, and relationships between parts—not ornament or complex imagery.
- Every component inherits the smoothness and refinement of the whole.
- Futurism appears as sophisticated capability organized without visual burden.
- Responsive intelligence feels immanent: present within the surface and close to the user's intention.
- That immanent responsiveness creates a transcendent or angelic impression without religious or biological imagery.
- The product must be easy to enter and easy to stay with.
## Color
- Primary: Aether Blue `#3657C9`.
- Secondary: Vesper Violet `#6F49D8`.
- Accent 1: Halo Cyan `#0087B8`.
- Accent 2: Aureate Amber `#8A5A13`.
- Signature white: `#FFFEFA`.
- Signature black: `#05070A`.
- Celestial is the default light environment: warm pearl/tan, clear work surfaces, cool or chromed blue, and restrained sunset reflection.
- Night is the dark environment: a visible blue surface ladder and controlled luminous accents for focused cyberspace.
- *Neuromancer* is a Night-only reference for inhabitable cyberspace and concentrated technical flow, never for grime, danger, clutter, neon spectacle, or hacker decoration.
- Night is independently composed and validated; it is not an inversion of Celestial.
- The reusable spectrum contains red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, violet, and magenta families.
- Every chromatic family has tones 1–10.
- `light`, `normal`, and `strong` resolve to tones 3, 5, and 8.
- Semantic roles consume the palette instead of inventing independent colors.
- Named spatial gradients are separate from tonal ramps.
## Typography
- IBM Plex Sans is the Taurus product-interface face.
- IBM Plex Mono is the Taurus technical/interface-monospace face.
- Editor content retains its own document, spreadsheet, deck, or imported typography.
## Geometry and motion
- softened outer contours support approachability and continuity;
- exact internal grids support legibility and high-science precision;
- arbitrary blobs, excessive pills, and decorative diagonals are rejected;
- the interface is still at rest;
- motion exists only to explain change and must always be smooth;
- reduced motion preserves all meaning;
- Celestial and Night share identical geometry, layout, disclosure, resource signatures, motion verbs, durations, and easing. Only color tokens change.

# Taurus Alpha implementation relationship
Taurus Alpha remains the current implementation evidence. Yesod now governs:
```plain text
Founder intent / research / validated user evidence
    ↓
Governing Taurus Yesod document
    ↓
Canonical semantic token or interaction contract
    ↓
Taurus Alpha implementation-ready style mirror
    ↓
Code, components, and resource-specific aliases
    ↓
Screenshots + interaction + accessibility validation
    ↓
Findings recorded back in Yesod
```
- Yesod governs experience, semantic roles, and canonical token intent.
- Alpha's style documentation mirrors Yesod in implementation-ready form.
- Alpha code realizes and tests that mirror.
- implementation constraints and test evidence flow back to Yesod.
This documentation pass does not modify Taurus Alpha code.

# Research basis
The canon is informed by:
- [Processing fluency and aesthetic pleasure](https://doi.org/10.1207/S15327957PSPR0804_3)
- [Visual complexity and prototypicality in first impressions](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhcs.2012.06.003)
- [Measuring visual clutter](https://doi.org/10.1167/7.2.17)
- [W3C consistent visual design pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o1p03-consistent-design/)
- [The restorative benefits of nature](https://doi.org/10.1016/0272-4944(95)90001-2)
- [Humans Prefer Curved Visual Objects](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01759.x)
- [Color Psychology](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010213-115035)
- [Color and emotion: hue, saturation, and brightness](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-017-0880-8)
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)
These sources guide hypotheses and constraints. They do not replace testing with realistic Taurus work.
